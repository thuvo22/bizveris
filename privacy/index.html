Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Who we are

BizVeris provides revenue diagnostics for home service businesses. This policy covers bizveris.com, our audit intake forms, and the opt in pages we host at bizveris.com/optin/ on behalf of the businesses we work with.

Information we collect

  • Contact information you provide: name, phone number, email address and business name.
  • Project details you submit through a form, such as the work you need done.
  • Consent records: when you check a box agreeing to receive text messages, we record the date and time, the page you were on, your IP address and browser, and the exact wording you agreed to.
  • Business data a client business connects for its own diagnostic, which is handled under our agreement with that business and is never sold.

Text messaging

When you submit a form on an opt in page we host, you are consenting to receive text messages from the business named on that page, at the number you provided. Those messages are service related: estimate follow ups, appointment reminders, payment receipts and review requests.

  • Consent is not a condition of purchase.
  • Message frequency varies depending on your project and inquiry.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to any message to opt out, or HELP for help. You can also opt out by contacting the business directly using the number on its page.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  • No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt in data and consent are never sold or shared with any third party.

How we use information

  • To respond to your request and provide an estimate.
  • To send the service related messages described above.
  • To keep a record of consent, so we can prove a message was permitted.
  • To operate and improve our services.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We share it only with:

  • The business you contacted through its opt in page, so it can serve you.
  • Service providers that operate our systems, such as messaging and hosting providers, under contract and only for that purpose.
  • Legal authorities when required by law.

Data security and retention

Access to connected business systems is read only wherever the provider supports it, and we do not store banking credentials. We keep consent records for as long as messaging continues plus the period required to demonstrate compliance. We take reasonable measures to protect information, though no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Your rights

You may request access to the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or opt out of communications at any time. Contact us at onpoint.pros.tx@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new revision date.
