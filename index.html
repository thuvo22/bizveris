What this looks like in practice

The first one is my own company. The other two are typical builds, shown as examples.

Running today

Remodeling company in Texas (the founder's own)

Situation: Leads from ads, forms, phone and referrals, each handled a different way. Bottleneck: Follow-up and reporting depended on the owner. Workflows: Instant reply to every new lead, a daily follow-up queue with drafted messages, scoring of every sales call, replies to reviews, and matching of payments to jobs. Result: The owner reviews and approves instead of doing the work by hand.

Example build

HVAC company, 14 staff

Situation: Office staff copy every call into the CRM and the schedule. Bottleneck: After-hours calls wait until morning. Workflows: Missed-call text-back, automatic job creation from calls and forms, a morning dispatch summary. Target: 40 to 60 hours a month back, and no lead waiting overnight.

Example build

Plumbing company, 11 staff

Situation: The office takes every call by hand and writes estimates at night. Bottleneck: Emergency calls after hours go to voicemail, and estimates sit for days with no follow-up. Workflows: After-hours text-back that collects the address and the problem, estimate follow-ups on a set schedule, review requests sent when a job is marked complete. Target: 30 to 50 hours a month back, and every estimate followed up within two days.

Questions owners ask first

Do we need a specific tech stack?

No. We build around what you already use: your CRM, phone system, email, calendar, spreadsheets and accounting. If a tool has no way to connect, we tell you on the audit, before you pay anything.

How secure is our data?

We ask for the least access each workflow needs, use your own accounts where possible, and never use your data to train public AI models. Access is listed in writing and you can revoke it at any time.

What if our processes are a mess?

Most are. Mapping the mess is the first step of the day, and often the most useful one. We automate the parts that are stable and flag the parts that need a decision from you first.

What if we don't like the workflows?

You test each one with us before it goes live, and we adjust it on the spot. After launch, changes are part of the 30-day check-in. If one isn't helping, we replace it.

Can your system break things we already have running?

We build next to your current setup, not on top of it. Each workflow can be paused with one switch. Anything that sends a message to a customer starts in approval mode, so a person signs off until you trust it.

How long until we see value?

The first workflows are live by the end of the Upgrade Day. Most teams feel the difference in the first week. You get measured hours at the 30-day check-in.

Do you work with businesses outside the U.S.?

Yes, if your team works in English and your tools are standard cloud software. The Upgrade Day is fully remote.

Is the audit call a sales pitch?

It is a working session. We estimate your wasted hours with you and name the two or three workflows worth building. If we're not a fit, we say so on the call.
